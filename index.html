Hang Jiang 江航

I’m an Assistant Professor at Northeastern University, jointly appointed in the D’Amore-McKim School of Business and the Khoury College of Computer Sciences. I direct the Human-AI Nexus Group.

I study how AI represents people, how it shapes communication across groups, and how people and AI agents work together in organizations. My research draws on natural language processing (NLP), human-computer interaction (HCI), and computational social science (CSS).

Research

Social Simulation & Representation

Whose perspectives does AI capture, and whose does it miss? We compare simulations of individuals and communities with evidence from the people they represent.

HugAgent · PersonaLLM · CommunityLM · Social Simulation Arena (preprint)

AI-Mediated Communication

People can use the same word and mean different things. We build tools that make those differences visible, help journalists interpret audience feedback, and bring community perspectives into civic decisions.

Bridging Dictionary · AudienceView · Human-AI Narrative Synthesis

Human-AI Collaboration & the Future of Work

We study how individuals practice interpersonal skills with AI and how organizations change their workflows as people and AI work together.

FaciliTrain · Prelude (ongoing) · AI-native organizations (ongoing)

AI Agents & Responsible Evaluation

When an agent acts on someone’s behalf, what counts as success? We investigate whether agents follow rules over long workflows and how their decisions affect access to opportunities.

χ-Bench · Agent-mediated hiring
