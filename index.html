AI at the nexus of people, work, and society.

I study how people and AI think, work, communicate, and organize together—and how we can build AI systems that support human agency, collaboration, and social understanding.

I am an Assistant Professor with joint appointments in the Khoury College of Computer Sciences and the D’Amore-McKim School of Business at Northeastern University.

Research

Understanding—and shaping—life with AI.

My work brings together computational social science, natural language processing, human–AI interaction, and AI agents.

01

Human–AI collaboration and AI-native organizations

How people and AI coordinate work, share judgment, and form new kinds of organizations.

02

Communication and work

AI-mediated communication, work, and organizational behavior—from interpersonal interaction to institutional practice.

03

Socially interactive AI systems

Responsible systems that understand people, communities, and social context while preserving human agency.
