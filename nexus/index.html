Our research

Where technical systems meet human life.

We combine computational methods with human-centered inquiry to understand emerging relationships among people, AI systems, teams, and institutions.

01

Human–AI collaboration and AI-native organizations

How shared work, judgment, and coordination change when AI becomes an active collaborator.

02

AI-mediated communication and the future of work

How AI reshapes conversation, interpersonal dynamics, creative practice, and organizational life.

03

Socially interactive and responsible AI

Systems that respond to social context while protecting agency, pluralism, and human values.

04

Computational social science, NLP, and AI agents

New methods for studying behavior and building systems that engage with complex social worlds.
